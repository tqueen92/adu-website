Amsterdam Data Union

Real data problems. Honest answers. Vendor neutral.

A trusted network, not another meetup.

Most data events show polished success stories. We compare how different companies solve the same problem, and what did not work. Just practitioners talking openly.

Who's in the room

A small, curated group of experienced data people from data engineering, analytics, data science, AI and ML, and data product management. Many lead data teams. Everyone shares, everyone learns.

Questions we get.

Is it recorded?

No streaming and no video. After some events, we share the key takeaways, always checked with the speakers first.

Is it free?

Yes, unless the event invitation says otherwise.

Can I speak or host an event?

Yes. Send us a message via Contact us.

What happens if I register and can't make it?

Let us know via Contact us, so we can offer your place to someone else.

Want to join, speak or host?

Tell us who you are and what you work on. We read every message and reply personally.
