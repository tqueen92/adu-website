A trusted network, not another meetup.

ADU is a small, curated network of experienced data people. Everyone in the room can share their experience and learn from others. That is what makes it work.

Members come from data engineering, analytics, data science, AI and ML, and data product management. Many of them lead data teams.

Members learn how other companies handle real data challenges, build trusted relationships across companies, and find career opportunities, talent and introductions through the network.

Vendor-free. No sponsors, no sales pitches.

Two ways we meet.

Every 2 to 3 months

Panel discussions

Speakers from companies of different sizes and industries explain how they solved the same data challenge. This is where the learning happens.

2 to 3 times a year

Networking drinks

Casual after-work drinks in Amsterdam. No agenda, no slides. This is where trust and real relationships grow.

In the room.

Questions we get.

Is it recorded?

No streaming and no video. We record audio for our own notes and never share it without the speaker's OK. Slides are shared afterwards if the speaker agrees.

Is it free?

Yes, unless the event invitation says otherwise.

Can I speak or host an event?

Yes. Email us at amsterdam.dataunion@gmail.com.

What happens if I register and can't make it?

Let one of the organizers know, so we can offer your place to someone else.

Want to join, speak or host?

Tell us who you are and what you work on. We read every email and reply personally.
